- [stack] API in Python 3.14 + FastAPI in `api/` (task allows only Java 21+/Python 3.14+ and requires REST + Docker); Next.js stays only as the optional frontend
- [stack] No Supabase/Vercel/DB (task bans external SaaS/APIs and storing files; nothing needs persistence)
- [ai] Default provider: Eden AI (`deepinfra/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash`, open weights, text + images, JSON schema; since 2026-11-22: Qwen3-VL-30B-A3B looped and wrote ~15 tokens/s; before `deepinfra/Qwen/Qwen3-VL-30B-A3B-Instruct`), also in the delivered solution (team decision 2026-11-21: faster and better than the 8B model on CPU). Its thinking is turned off with `reasoning_effort: none` (default `high`); the Qwen `/no_think` switch is added only for Qwen models. Offline option: local Ollama, `qwen3-vl:8b` (Apache-2.0, reads text and scans; small = cheap to deploy). Answer limit 12000 tokens (form 9 needed ~7.6k and hit 8000)
- [ai] Eden AI (organizer-provided) is the only external AI provider, open-weight models only (same model must run locally); no Anthropic API (task rules). Chosen with `AUTOFORMULARZ_LLM_PROVIDER=eden` (plain httpx, no SDK). `docker-compose.yml` runs the API on Eden by default (`ollama` and `model` only with `--profile ollama` and `AUTOFORMULARZ_LLM_PROVIDER=ollama`); the eden overlay file is gone. Key only in `api`. Cost per call in `llm.EDEN_USAGE` (results reports, total since start in `/health`); extra seams: `llm.before_eden_call` (can block a call) and `llm.usage_hooks` (token usage per paid response). Ollama stays on `qwen3-vl:8b` (another family than the Eden model: answers may differ between providers); the previous dev pair was `deepinfra/Qwen/Qwen3-VL-30B-A3B-Instruct` ↔ `qwen3-vl:30b`
- [ai] Model test 2026-11-22 behind the choice of DeepSeek V4.1 Flash (the `-1017` checkpoint, same results): 6 open-weight models on form 9 (the hardest) and the winner on forms 1-8: fewest places to fill in left without a field, no made-up statements, no repetition loop on any form, 3-24 s and under 0.005 USD per form (Qwen3-VL-30B-A3B, the model before: 1-7 min, loops, made-up statements; Qwen3.5-397B close but 9x the cost; Qwen3.6-35B and Qwen3.5-122B always reason first, which cannot be switched off through Eden, and the 122B used its whole limit on it; Mistral-Small-4-119B returned invalid JSON). Open weights, MIT (`deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash-1017`), a 552B MoE with 8B active: runs on a GPU server, not a laptop. Offline option: local Ollama, `qwen3-vl:8b` (Apache-2.0, reads text and scans; small = cheap to deploy). Answer limit 12000 tokens (form 9 needed ~7.6k and hit 8000)
- [ai] LLM fills a small FormSpec (constrained by JSON Schema); a deterministic compiler builds the COI JSON (long JSON with UUIDs/jsonata from a small model is unreliable; code is testable and repeatable)
- [ai] Predefined sections (office, applicant, address, proxy, stamp duty, contact, consent, preview) come from compiler templates, not the model (standard says use them; same techNames and queries as reference JSON)
- [ai] "Podmiot wniosku" follows the standard (p. 10, "Ważne"): a form with only a natural person's data offers "Swoim" and "Innej osoby" (other person's data, power of attorney, stamp duty), with company data also "Osoby prawnej…". So a ZUS Z-15 gets these sections although its paper does not mention a proxy, like the COI references 1 and 5. A rule that dropped them without a proxy on paper was tried on 2026-11-22 and reverted (against the standard)
- [format] A period from-to is `DATE_RANGE` in FormSpec and two DATEPICKER fields "… – od" (required as on paper) and "… – do" (optional: a period may be one day) in the COI JSON, which has no range type. The canon also turns a TEXT/DATE field into a range when its label or description says "od–do" / "od dnia … do dnia" together with a date word (data, dni, okres, termin); "godziny od-do" stays text
- [format] Fewer steps: a page with fewer than 3 fields to fill becomes sections of the previous page (the first page: of the next one), as the last canon step. On the saved results: "Załączniki" (forms 2, 7, 8), "Oświadczenie" (8) and "Rodzaj wniosku" (9) are no longer steps of their own, lint still 0 errors. The prompt asks for pages as stages with several sections (data of several persons on one page, optional sections with `show_if`)
- [format] Follow the reference JSON where it differs from the written schema: `repeatingSectionGrid` (not `repetingSectionGrid`), also for plain SECTION; address/contact prefilled from MLPZ but editable
- [format] FINISH page may hold INFO_SECTION and TEXT_BLOCK (agreed with COI mentors; reference 5 uses TEXT_BLOCK), no warning
- [format] Verbatim source text only for instructions and legal basis (TEXT_BLOCK sections on the FINISH page); statements stay short and gender-neutral from the model, the GDPR clause is only linked (design standard, "Oświadczenie"). Legal basis always on, checked in code (`compiler.legal_basis`): text after a "Podstawa prawna" label or heading, the part of the request sentence before "wnoszę/proszę" that cites a provision ("Na podstawie art. 75 …", "Zgodnie z ustawą …"), or a block the model marked LEGAL_BASIS that cites a provision (art., §, ustawa, rozporządzenie, kodeks, Dz.U.) and is not a first-person statement; never the GDPR clause. On the materials: forms 2, 5, 7 and 8 get it, the others have none on paper. Instructions ON by default too (`AUTOFORMULARZ_COPY_SOURCE_TEXT=true`), checked in code per item (a numbered point or a sentence over several lines; `compiler.instruction_texts`): the items after a "Pouczenie:" label on the paper, and the model's INSTRUCTION items that tell the applicant something the e-form does not (fees and where to pay, deadlines, duties, consequences, how the case is handled). Never references to paper positions or ways of filling it in ("kratkę TAK w poz. 34", "Objaśnienie dotyczące punktu B.1.2", "niepotrzebne skreślić"; a journal's "Dz. U. … poz. 132" is not one), a field's hint in brackets, attachment notes, the request or a statement. On the saved results: forms 3 (fee, bank account, exemption) and 8 (deadline, fee, enforcement notice, who must file) get them; the model's other INSTRUCTION roles are dropped
- [ai] Presort (`presort.py`, `AUTOFORMULARZ_PRESORT`, on): code recognises the GDPR clause, paper-only lines (signature, date, "wypełnia urząd", fill-in instructions, page footers) and the applicant/address/contact data the compiler adds from the template; those blocks are left out of the prompt and their roles added after the model (fewer input and output tokens, fewer role errors). Rules on normalised text (no diacritics, no numbering) so wording may vary; when unsure the block goes to the model. Hand-written lines (`……`, `____`) are shortened to `……` in the prompt only. Runs of ≥6 blocks identical to an earlier run (later versions of a multi-version request, form 9) are shortened to a note "dalej bloki … takie same jak …", so the model describes the common part once; form 8's short repeats (rate tables E.1/E.2) stay. Measured on the materials: form 8 prompt −30%, form 9 −57%, forms 1 and 7 −60–80%
- [ai] A looping answer is not an error: Ollama streams the answer and generation stops when the model writes 2 sections in a row that it already wrote (same title and fields, as in the canon); an answer cut at the limit (Eden, or Ollama at `llm_max_tokens`) is salvaged too: its complete pages/sections up to the loop are used, with a note in the result, no second call. A single repeated section is normal (form 9: the same "Opinia" on two pages) and never stops the answer; checked on all saved results
- [ai] Answer size per document (`llm.model_limits`, `llm.answer_budget`): the enforced schema gets `maxItems` for pages, sections per page, fields per section, options, statements and block refs, and `max_tokens` grows with the blocks sent (2000 + 100 per block or table cell, at least 3000; `AUTOFORMULARZ_LLM_MAX_TOKENS` stays the ceiling). A small form (form 1: 7 blocks -> 4 pages, 8 sections, 12 fields, 3000 tokens instead of 15/40/12000) cannot loop for long even when the detectors miss it. Every saved result 2-9 fits its limits with room (test). The schema in the Eden prompt stays the static one, so the system prompt is the same for every form (prompt cache). A call we stop on a loop is recorded as `finishReason: "repeat"` (the provider then sends no usage: cost unknown)
- [ai] Versions of one request (form 9): when the canon drops a copy of a section, it merges the copies' `show_if` (union over the options; not expressible as one condition = always shown), and a section whose source blocks repeat in another version (shortened by presort) is shown for that version too. Before, the model's per-version copies collapsed to the first one and the "Nagroda" form was empty
- [ai] Ollama `keep_alive` 30m (`AUTOFORMULARZ_LLM_KEEP_ALIVE`, Ollama's default 5m): the next form does not reload the model and reuses the cached system prompt
- [format] Consents (`consents`, CONSENT_SECTION as the design standard requires) are required, except a consent to be contacted by e-mail/phone/electronic delivery (the contact data are optional, so it cannot block sending); paper references like "wskazanego w poz. 71" are removed from their text
- [lint] Field order for condition checks comes from grid `y`/`x`, not the JSON array order (reference files store fields out of display order)
- [format] Element ids are UUIDv5 from form title + path (same input gives the same JSON, readable diffs)
- [format] `validation` lists REQUIRED + validators (PESEL, NIP, REGON, ZIP_CODE, EMAIL...); validators also inferred from labels (reference leaves it empty; validation is an extra-points item)
- [extract] DOCX read from XML incl. text boxes (forms 2 and 8 are built from text boxes); .ods read natively from its XML (same output as the LibreOffice path on all 9 materials); only binary .doc via LibreOffice headless (MPL-2.0 external process, not linked, nothing of ours to publish; mentors: allowed only with such a license, be careful); scanned PDF pages rendered with pypdfium2 (PyMuPDF is AGPL)
- [extract] Scans: tall images of stacked pages are cut into A4 pages at blank rows; OCR with Tesseract + `pol` (Apache-2.0, separate process, stdin, one thread) gives the same Document blocks as text files, page images still go to the model (OCR misses empty tick boxes). Deterministic, ~1 s/page, 87-100% word recall vs DOCX on the materials; without Tesseract scans go as images only
- [extract] Page images for the model: longer side 1280 px, JPEG quality 80 (was PNG 1600 px). OCR still reads the full-resolution page. On the material scans: ~37% fewer image tokens per page (2280 -> 1440) and 60-90% less data to send; not yet checked with the model whether empty tick boxes are still seen
- [ai] Model schema allows no default values: no null, lists with at least one item, flags only as the non-default value (`model_schema()`); left-out keys get Pydantic defaults. Saved specs are ~45% default values when every key is written, and the output tokens are the slowest step. FormSpec and the contract are unchanged; every saved result reads back identical without its defaults (test)
- [api] Metrics per conversion (`metrics.py`): steps, model calls (time, time to first token, tokens, cost, image tokens, nulls/[]/false in the raw answer), result size. One JSON log line, `metrics` in `?details=true`, `wyniki/metrics.jsonl` + `NN.raw.txt` from the result generator (not committed). File identified by a short SHA-256, never by name or text
- [upload] `.doc` and `.docx` are one format ("DOC (.doc .docx)" in the task): the content decides how it is read (materials 5 and 7 are DOCX named .DOC)
- [api] Contract for parallel work: `extract` returns a `Document` (blocks `b1…` + field candidates), FormSpec points to block IDs (`blocks`, `block_roles`), `?details=true` adds `document` and `coverage` (model classifies, code keeps the text; see docs/pipeline.md "Kontrakt")
- [api] `POST /api/v1/forms` returns the importable JSON as-is; `?details=true` adds FormSpec, lint issues and notes for the UI
- [api] Progress via polled jobs: `POST /api/v1/jobs` returns 202 + id, `GET /api/v1/jobs/{id}` gives the steps so far (pipeline stages, then title/page/section/field names read from the streamed model answer) and the result, `DELETE` cancels (the stream closes, the model stops). Jobs only in process memory, dropped 15 min after the last change, max 4 running. Reason: one long request hit fetch's 5-minute header timeout in the Next proxy; progress costs no tokens (no extra model calls, Eden streams with `stream_options.include_usage`). `POST /api/v1/forms` stays synchronous for other clients
- [api] Host lookups IPv4 first (`net.py`, installed at API start): in Docker Desktop on some networks the local DNS answers the IPv6 query for api.edenai.run (IPv4 only) with "no such domain", Docker keeps it a few seconds and the IPv4 lookup fails too (`[Errno -5] No address associated with hostname`): one of 3 forms sent at once failed with LLM_UNAVAILABLE. httpx has no address-family setting, so `socket.getaddrinfo` is wrapped: IPv4 first, the combined lookup only without an IPv4 address. Measured in the container: 30/30 and 24/24 at once (combined: most failed); 6 forms 3 at a time: 0 errors. A connect error to Eden is also retried after 1, 2, 4, 8 s (`llm.connect_with_retry`; nothing was sent, so it costs nothing); read errors and timeouts are not retried (the model may already have answered, paid)

One row per file (queued, current stage, done with rule/safety counts, error), preview and checks of the chosen row under the list, "Pobierz wszystkie" saves one ZIP built in the browser (`src/lib/zip.ts`, stored, no dependency: Safari blocks several downloads from one click, Chrome holds them behind a prompt; two sources with one name keep their extension, "wniosek.docx.json"), "Ponów nieudane" runs failed and cancelled files again; cancel stops running jobs on the server (DELETE) and the queued ones, finished forms stay. No backend change: one file stays the single-file flow
- [ui] Safety scan of the generated form (`src/lib/form/safety.ts`, no dependency): requests for passwords/PIN/SMS codes/card data, links outside gov.pl or without HTTPS, text addressed to a model (prompt injection, EN and PL), hidden zero-width/bidi characters, HTML/script, labels over 200 characters. It warns (list in the check step, confirmation before "Pobierz JSON") and never changes the JSON; no false alarms on wyniki 2-9

The model's own SKIP roles are not trusted for this (form 6: it gave SKIP to the contractor's phone). Checked on wyniki 2-9: no real field lost

Form 1 had "Numer porządkowy" 22 times and ran to the 12000-token limit (~7 min)

"Numer PESEL" in "Twoje dane" and "Dane innej osoby" only when the paper asks for a PESEL (presort, outside the GDPR clause): the design standard lists it, but both COI references follow the paper (material 1 and its visualisation have none, material 5 has it)

Output: `dictionaries` in `?details=true`, `proposedDictionaries` in reports, `wyniki/slowniki.json`
- [ai] Prompt injection through documents: hidden Word runs (`w:vanish`) and zero-width/bidi characters and soft hyphens are dropped at extraction; the document's blocks sit between `<<<DOKUMENT` and `DOKUMENT>>>` with a system-prompt rule that they are data, never orders (such blocks get SKIP), each block on one line with the markers defused; `safety.py` scans every result (same rules as `src/lib/form/safety.ts`) and adds `SAFETY_*` warnings to `issues`, so API clients see them too (the web preview shows them once, from its own scan). - [extract] OCR in two passes: Tesseract `--psm 4` (one column, keeps form rows) drops the words after a long dotted leader, so a `--psm 11` (sparse text) pass adds what it found and the first pass did not: into the same row after "……" (a place to fill in), or as a new row in place; a row read a little differently by both passes is not doubled, words below confidence 60 or without 3 letters are ignored. Scan of material 1 now reads "w miejscowości …… przy ulicy" and "nr ewidencyjny działki …… w obrębie" like the PDF; form 9's table labels and form 3's "inne" come back. - [ai] Statements the model writes stay only when most of their words occur in the document (the prompt's example "Dane w formularzu są zgodne z prawdą" appeared on forms without statements); a choice field that repeats a statement already among the consents is dropped (form 7 had them twice, the field's condition pointing to itself)
- [ai] Best of N (`AUTOFORMULARZ_LLM_SAMPLES`, default 1 for the live API): the model answers N times (the first at temperature 0, the others at `llm_sample_temperature` 0.4) and `pipeline.score` keeps the answer with the fewest lint errors, then the fewest places to fill in left without a field, then the fewest elements the canon dropped, then the most fields
- [format] A statement the paper itself makes conditional is optional: 4 consecutive lines hold most of its words and "dotyczy wyłącznie/tylko", "jeżeli" or "jeśli" (form 8, D.4 compost bin: "dotyczy wyłącznie właścicieli…", "zaznaczają kratkę, jeżeli…"); GDPR blocks are blanked so a window never joins lines apart on paper. Set by presort (`FormSpec.optional_consents`, hidden from the model); on wyniki 2-9 only form 8's compost statement changes. Prompt: words split by a slash with a "niepotrzebne skreślić" mark ("wynajem/użyczenie*") are a single choice (form 4). The best-of-N score counts statements with fields

Wnioskodawca", sends it to the next part unless that is much farther; positions by the median of a section's blocks). Answers skip different things from run to run: form 9's best answer had the conditions per kind of request but not the club representative and proxy, another one the opposite; wyniki/09 keeps both (23 conditions, 0 lint errors)
